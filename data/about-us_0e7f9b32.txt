About us
Leading Sustainable Innovation and Technological Advancement in River Sand Mining.
OUR IDENTITY
At IIT Ropar, we focus on promoting responsible and sustainable practices in river sand mining through advanced geospatial and AI-driven technologies. Our collaborative initiative integrates research, innovation, and community engagement to balance developmental needs with ecological protection.

High-accuracy Digital Elevation Model creation and topographic analysis.
