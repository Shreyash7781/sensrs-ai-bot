Centre of Excellence
Socio-Environmental Sustainability for
River Sand-mining
The CoE SEnSRS at IIT Ropar, established by IIT Ropar with seed funding from the Government of Punjab, is dedicated to ensuring socio-environmental sustainability in river sand mining. We combine advanced geospatial technologies, field research, and policy engagement to safeguard communities and ecosystems while enabling responsible development.

Advanced Geospatial Technologies
Cutting-edge remote sensing and GIS for precise monitoring of sand mining activities and environmental impacts.
Comprehensive Field Research
Ground-truthing studies and ecological assessments to validate data and understand real-world impacts.
Policy Engagement
Partnering with institutions and communities to create responsible sand mining strategies for sustainable growth.
