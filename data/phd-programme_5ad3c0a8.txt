About the Centre
The Centre of Excellence in Socio-Environmental Sustainability for River Sand Mining (SEnSRS) at IIT Ropar is a pioneering initiative focused on interdisciplinary research, capacity building, and evidence-based policymaking for sustainable river resource management. Recognizing the challenges posed by unsustainable sand mining — including ecological degradation, hydrological changes, socio-economic impacts, and governance issues — the Centre aims to develop scientific solutions and policy interventions for long-term sustainability.
In alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, SEnSRS proposes to launch a Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Programme from January 2026. The programme will integrate engineering, environmental sciences, social sciences, law, and emerging technologies such as AI, GeoAI, and digital governance tools to address key aspects of river sand mining and riverine sustainability.
1. Research Areas
Research under the programme will focus on a wide range of thematic areas, including but not limited to:
River Geomorphology and Sediment Dynamics
Environmental Impacts and Ecological Sustainability of Sand Mining
Hydrological Modelling and River Basin Management
GIS, Remote Sensing, and Drone-based Monitoring Applications
Socio-Economic, Legal, and Policy Dimensions of River Resource Governance
Sustainability Indicators and Decision Support Systems
Artificial Intelligence (AI), GeoAI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning for Predictive Modelling
Hyperautomation and Workflow Intelligence for Environmental Data Processing
Web-based GIS Dashboards and Spatial Decision Support Portals
Mobile and Web Application Development for Real-Time River Monitoring
2. Selection Procedure
Eligible candidates possessing the minimum educational qualifications will be called for a
"test and interview"

3. Admission Procedure & Modes
(i) Regular Full-Time PhD
Candidates under this category are entitled to Centre Fellowship from SEnSRS.
(ii-a) Part-Time ERP
External Registration Program — for candidates employed in R&D organizations / academic institutions. Research may be carried out largely in the parent organization with overall IIT Ropar guidance.
(ii-b) Part-Time PhD
Research scholar carries out major part or all research work at IIT Ropar under supervision of IIT Ropar supervisors.
(ii-c) Self-Sponsored
Project staff are eligible for Part-time / Full-time PhD enrollment. *Special preference will be given to candidates nominated by the Punjab government under the Centre's scheme.
