DGPS Operational Strategy
Our DGPS surveying methodology centers on achieving absolute spatial precision using a network of high-frequency GNSS receivers. By establishing a fixed base station on a known geodetic control point, we compute and broadcast real-time differential corrections (RTK) to mobile rover units, eliminating satellite-derived errors and atmospheric interference.
This dual-receiver architecture ensures that every captured coordinate—whether for topographic mapping, dam structural monitoring, or highway corridor alignment—maintains centimeter-level accuracy. The seamless integration between field data collectors and advanced post-processing software allows us to deliver high-resolution DEMs, contour maps, and GIS layers that form the foundation of critical engineering and environmental assessments.
