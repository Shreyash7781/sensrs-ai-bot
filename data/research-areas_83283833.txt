Environmental Monitoring
Environmental Monitoring & Restoration
Unregulated sand extraction leads to riverbank erosion, groundwater depletion, biodiversity loss, and vegetation degradation. Using geospatial and field-based methods, we assess these impacts to design strategies for ecological restoration and long-term sustainability.
Project: Safe Depth of Mining Study
Researching permissible mining depths at out-of-riverbed sites using DGPS, UAV surveys, and chain-mounted data collectors, with findings shared in an upcoming technical report.
Policy & Governance
Governance, Policy & Legal Frameworks
Mining is not only a geological activity but also a policy and governance challenge. Our work examines existing policies, identifies gaps, and recommends transparent, community-centered frameworks such as benefit-sharing agreements and social license to operate models.
Project: Policy Impact Review for Punjab
Assessing the effectiveness of current policies, comparing governance of public vs. government mining sites, and proposing reforms to enhance accountability.
Tech & AI Monitoring
Technology & AI for Mining Monitoring
We leverage drones, GIS, satellite remote sensing, and AI-driven predictive analytics to ensure real-time monitoring of mining activities. These tools provide early warning for illegal mining and improve accuracy in District Survey Reports (DSRs).
Project: District Survey Report Vetting
IIT Ropar’s CoE has vetted multiple DSRs (SAS Nagar, Taran Taran, Ferozpur, Fazilka) and continues to review others using geospatial technologies for transparency and scientific accuracy.
Sustainable Practices
Sustainable Practices & Best Management Models
Our center advocates for ethical and sustainable sand mining by integrating scientific guidelines, community participation, and ecological safeguards. We promote frameworks where economic benefits are balanced with social and environmental responsibilities.
Project: Community Benefit Sharing Framework
Developing a model for fair revenue distribution and community compensation in Punjab, drawing on global best practices in sustainable mining.
